<h2 align="center">🔄 The Old Way vs This Way</h2>

<div align="center">

| 😩 The old way | ✅ With Collections Desk |
|:---|:---|
| 😩 Chasing every customer the same way | ✅ A ranked call list with urgent accounts first and broken promises flagged |
| 😩 No clear picture of collection health | ✅ A 0 to 100 health score with the reasons behind it |
| 😩 Ageing reports nobody acts on | ✅ Plain-language notes on who holds the overdue and where to start |
| 😩 Cash forecasts that are a guess | ✅ Expected cash for the next 7 and 30 days from each customer's payment habits |
| 😩 Rebuilding the call sheet every morning | ✅ One click to an Excel call list (full version) |

</div>

<h2 align="center">✨ What You Get</h2>

<table>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 🩺 Collection Health Score
One score from 0 to 100 built from overdue share, DSO against your target, share beyond 90 days and collection efficiency.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 📞 Ranked Call List
Who to call today, ordered by urgency, with notes on broken promises and the amount at stake.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 📊 Debtors Ageing
Clickable buckets for 0 to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90 and 90+ days, with average age, average delay and the oldest invoice.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 🔮 Cash Forecast
What is likely to arrive in the next 7 and 30 days, based on how each customer has paid before.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 👥 Customers & Team
Customer exposure and credit-limit breaches, plus branch and salesperson views of who is holding the cash.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 🧾 Invoice Register
Every open invoice in one register, filtered by branch, salesperson, ageing basis and your own DSO target.

</td>
</tr>
</table>
